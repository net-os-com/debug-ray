---
title: The menu bar at a glance
date: 2026-11-13
---

- The menu bar icon counts what arrived in the stream while you were not looking, and the
  number turns red when an exception is among it. Opening the panel or bringing the window
  forward clears it.
- The panel opens with the last request: method, path, status, how long it took and how
  many queries it ran, the duration in red when it was slow. Click it and the app opens on
  Requests with that request selected.
- Right-click the icon to pause or resume the stream, to clear it, and to download and
  install an update when there is one.
- Pausing is now one pause for the whole app, from the toolbar, the palette or the menu bar.
  While it holds, the icon dims and stops pulsing, and there are no banners and no "Focus
  on errors": everything waits in the list until you resume. A window opened while paused
  holds back the same events.
- Collected requests survive a closed window, as the stream already did, and their Clear
  sticks: a window opened again does not bring them back.
- With "Collect requests" off, requests are now dropped on arrival everywhere, so MCP
  clients no longer read them either.
