---
title: A new way around the app
date: 2026-10-15
---

- A navigation rail and a command palette (Cmd-K), built from the navigation canvas.
- Pick the Docker container in Settings, and be told plainly when the wrong one is
  chosen.
- The stream sorts newest or oldest first, and remembers which.
- The theme control moved to Settings.
- ERD: problems report on the badge, a hovered relation can be followed, and the editor
  links where it should.
- ERD: Landlord is now called Central throughout.
- Scrollbars follow the theme.
