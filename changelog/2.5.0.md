---
title: Mocks
date: 2026-11-20
---

- NetOS Debug can now stand in for a third-party API. It answers the platform's calls with
  fixed responses and fires webhooks at the platform, so an integration can be tested
  locally without that service's token.
- The responses live in the platform repository, as `mocks/<service>.yaml`, so the whole
  team shares them. The new Mocks view lists every service, its endpoints and its
  responses, and every call that reaches the mock server, with the response that answered
  it or the fact that none did.
- Opt in per developer by pointing a service's base URL in your own `.env` at NetOS Debug,
  for example `BALENA_API_URL=http://host.docker.internal:23517/mock/balena`. Restart the
  worker and the scheduler after changing `.env`: they keep the config in memory. The mock
  only answers while NetOS Debug is open; with it closed, calls fail to connect and never
  reach the real API.
- Switch the active response of an endpoint from the view. The choice is yours alone and
  stays out of the repository, so it never shows up as a git diff.
- Send a webhook by hand from the view. It is signed with the secret from the app's
  config at send time and only goes to the local stack: localhost, 127.0.0.1 or a `.test`
  host. The secret never enters a fixture.
- "Save as mock" on an outgoing call turns a real call, recorded by someone who has the
  token, into a fixture.
- Needs `net-os/laravel-netos-debug` `v0.3.0` in the application, for the new
  `netos-debug:mocks` command.
