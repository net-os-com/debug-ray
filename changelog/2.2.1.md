---
title: Two fixes behind the new pages
date: 2026-10-16
---

- Closing the app no longer ends in `Object has been destroyed`. The realtime socket is
  closed along with everything else, and every message the main process sends now checks
  that the window is still there — frames were simply what happened to be arriving.
- HTTP: the verb goes into Copy as cURL quoted, and is checked on arrival. A payload
  claiming a method of `GET'; rm -rf ~; echo '` produced a command that read as three,
  and that command exists to be pasted into a terminal.
